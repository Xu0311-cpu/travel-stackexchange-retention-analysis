AI may reduce job opportunities in some areas because it can replace routine tasks. Many companies use AI tools to handle simple and repetitive work faster and cheaper than humans. For example, chatbots can answer basic customer questions, so fewer entry-level support staff are needed. AI can also automate data entry and simple reporting, which may reduce demand for junior office workers. As a result, some people, especially new graduates, may find it harder to get their first job. In the short term, this can increase pressure in the job market.
