# English Sentence Bank (Writing)

## Openings
1. This essay discusses both views and gives my opinion.
2. AI is changing the job market in different ways.

## View A: AI reduces jobs
3. AI can replace routine tasks, so some jobs may disappear.
4. For example, companies may need fewer workers for simple customer service.
5. As a result, some people may find it harder to get entry-level jobs.

## View B: AI creates jobs
6. AI can also create new roles and industries.
7. For instance, more people are needed to manage, test, and improve AI systems.
8. In addition, AI can help small teams do more, which may create new business opportunities.

## My opinion / balance
9. In my view, AI will change jobs rather than “kill” all jobs.
10. The key is education and training, so people can move to new roles.
11. Governments and companies should support reskilling programs.

## Conclusion
12. Overall, AI brings both risks and opportunities, but we can reduce the risks with good preparation.
