# Resume Bullets

## Version A — Balanced / Most Recommended
**Community Growth & Retention Analytics | Travel Stack Exchange (Real-world public data)**  
- Built a behavioral analytics pipeline from **414K+ real community events**, covering **40.6K users** and **214.9K user-days** from **2011–2024**
- Measured **DAU, D1 retention, monthly activity structure, and action-level retention**, identifying that **community scale peaks did not coincide with retention peaks**, revealing divergence between growth and stickiness
- Segmented participation into **question, comment-only, and answer behaviors**, and found that **answer-related contribution showed the highest next-day retention (~42%)**, compared with **26.5% for comment-only** and **22.6% for question**
- Validated action-level differences using **large base sizes, bootstrap confidence intervals, and cross-stage comparisons**, framing community quality as a **behavioral-depth problem rather than an activity-volume problem**

## Version B — More Business / Growth Oriented
**Community Retention & User Behavior Analysis | Travel Stack Exchange**  
- Processed **414K+ user actions** into a structured user-day dataset to analyze engagement and retention across **40K+ users**
- Quantified **DAU, D1 retention, monthly trends, and stage-level behavior differences**, uncovering a structural gap between **community growth** and **user stickiness**
- Discovered that **high-value contribution behavior (answering)** consistently aligned with the strongest next-day retention (**~42%**) across early, middle, and late community stages
- Translated behavioral analytics into a product-growth narrative by distinguishing **demand expression, light interaction, and content contribution** as separate retention mechanisms

## Version C — More Data / Analytics Oriented
**Behavioral Retention Analysis on Real Community Data | Travel Stack Exchange**  
- Parsed public XML dumps and built a **user-day behavioral dataset** with **414K+ events**, **40.6K users**, and **214.9K user-days**
- Developed a reproducible analytics workflow covering **retention measurement, time-series structure, behavioral segmentation, confidence intervals, and stage-level comparison**
- Found that **answer-oriented contribution** had the strongest and most stable D1 retention (**42.0%, 95% CI: 41.6%–42.5%**), substantially above **comment-only (26.5%)** and **question (22.6%)**
- Demonstrated the ability to connect **raw behavioral data → product metrics → stable insight → business interpretation**
